[MODULE_10] // The 5E Model

Constructivist Record

[REPORT]: Constructivism posits that learners build knowledge based on experience. In my Science lessons, I utilize the '5E Model' (Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate). For example, when teaching 'Photosynthesis' (Botany), I did not start with a definition. Instead, I took students to the school garden (Engage/Explore), asked them to observe leaf orientation, and then facilitated a discussion on why plants need sunlight. This record documents such lesson plans where students are active participants in their own learning, rather than passive recipients of facts.
